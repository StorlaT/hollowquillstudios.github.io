Goblin's Dice Stump Privacy Policy

Hollowquill Studios created Goblin’s Dice Stump as a fantasy-themed dice collection and organization app for tabletop gaming enthusiasts, collectors, and hobbyists.

We believe software should be transparent, respectful, and trustworthy. This page explains what information the app may collect and how it is used.

Information We Collect

Goblin’s Dice Stump may collect limited information required for app functionality and stability, including:

  • Basic analytics information
  • Crash reports and diagnostics
  • Cloud backup data (if enabled)
  • Dice collection images uploaded by the user

Most collection data is stored locally on your device unless optional cloud synchronization or backup features are enabled by the user.

Camera and Photos

Goblin’s Dice Stump may request access to your device camera and photo library so you can:

  • Take photos of your dice sets
  • Upload dice images into your collection
  • Manage and organize your dice inventory

Images are used solely for app functionality and are never sold to third parties.

Analytics and Crash Reporting

The app uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to help improve app stability, usability, and performance.

These services may collect anonymized technical information, such as device type, operating system version, app version, and crash logs.

Cloud Storage

If cloud backup or synchronization features are enabled, collection data and uploaded images may be securely stored using Firebase services.

Cloud features are optional and may require a user account.

Data Sharing

Hollowquill Studios does not sell personal information, uploaded images, or collection data to third parties.

Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of cloud-stored account data by contacting support@hollowquillstudios.com.

Locally stored collection data may also be removed directly from the app using built-in delete or reset functionality.

Children's Privacy

Goblin’s Dice Stump is not directed toward children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you may contact:
