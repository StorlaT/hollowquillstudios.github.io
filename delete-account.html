Goblin's Dice Stump - Account & Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their Goblin's Dice Stump account and associated data by emailing:

Email: support@hollowquillstudios.com

Please include the email address associated with your Goblin's Dice Stump account.

What will be deleted

  • Your app account/authentication data
  • Your cloud-synced dice collection data
  • Uploaded dice images associated with your account, where applicable

Processing time

Deletion requests are typically processed within 7 business days.

Data that may be retained

Some limited records may be retained where required for legal, security, fraud prevention, or billing purposes.
